NEET Live Photo Aadhaar Se Match Nahi Ho Raha — Kya Karein?

Form bharte waqt live photo wali screen par hazaron students atak jate hain — camera khulta hai, photo kheenchti hai, aur system bol deta hai: photo Aadhaar se match nahi hui. Ghabrao mat — iski wajah bhi pata hai aur ilaaj bhi. Ek-ek karke samjho.

🤔 Match Fail Hota Kyun Hai?

  • Sabse badi wajah — purani Aadhaar photo. Aadhaar me aksar 10–12 saal purani, bachpan ki photo hoti hai. Chehra badal gaya, to system do chehron ko ek insaan nahi manta.
  • Live photo ki galtiyan — andhera, chehre par saaya, chehra bahut chhota ya side me, sir jhuka hua, munh khula, peeche bheed-bhaad wala background, dhundhli photo.
  • Photo of photo — screen ya print ki photo kheenchna bhi fail karta hai; live photo hamesha asal chehre ki honi chahiye.

✅ Ilaaj — 3 Steps

1
Form se PEHLE ghar par practice karo.
PhotoTaiyaar bilkul form jaisa mahol deta hai — camera kholo, chehra ghare me rakho, aur 15 cheezon ki jaanch live hoti hai: chehra bada hai, dono kaan dikh rahe, munh band, nazar camera par, roshni barabar, background saada. Aadhaar wali photo se milan ka andaza bhi milega. Bilkul free, koi limit nahi — jitni baar chaho practice karo.
2
Form par sahi jagah, sahi roshni me baitho.
Khidki ke saamne ya safed light ke neeche, peeche halki-saadi deewar. Chehra camera ke bilkul saamne, dono kaan dikhe, munh band, chehra normal. Cap/topi hata do. Ek baar fail ho to ghabra kar baar-baar try mat karo — pehle practice wala step dobara karo.
3
Phir bhi atak jaye to do pakke raaste hain.
(a) Aadhaar me photo update — nazdeeki Aadhaar kendra/CSC par nayi photo lagwa lo, phir form bharo. (b) Annexure-VI pehchan praman patra — NEET UG 2026 me jinke liye eKYC/live-match sambhav nahi tha, unke liye ye raasta diya gaya tha (adhikari se pramanit certificate). Apne saal ke official bulletin me iski pushti zaroor kar lena.
📸 Abhi Free Practice Shuru Karo
⚠️ Ek imandari wali baat: asal match UIDAI/NTA apne band system se karta hai — koi bhi tool "ye photo pakka pass hogi" nahi keh sakta. Jo aisa dawa kare, usse bachna. PhotoTaiyaar galtiyan pehle pakadta hai aur andaza deta hai — guarantee nahi.
